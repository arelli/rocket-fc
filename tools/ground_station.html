Battery and charging (flight computer)

—
The XIAO has no charge-status signal. "Charging" means a computer is connected to the flight computer's USB with a cell present; "charged" means the voltage has stopped rising at about 4.2 V. While charging, the voltage reads higher than the resting voltage, so the percentage reads high. Charge current is 50–100 mA: a 700 mAh cell takes roughly 7–14 h.

Remote test fire by radio (ejection charge ground test)

This fires the real ejection charge from a distance. It works only when the flight computer is in bench mode, in PAD with igniter continuity (arm link fitted), and hears the ground station. Arm, then fire within 10 s. While armed the rocket beeps continuously and its LED blinks fast. In flight mode the rocket always refuses. Follow your club's or national rules for ejection tests.
